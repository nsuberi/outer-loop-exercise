# Agent instructions

## Don't spoil the workshop

This repo is a workshop exercise with two planted customer issues, each tied to its own complaint:

- `workshop/jira/FOOD-142.md`: a bug ticket filed through the official support system.
- `workshop/social/reddit-back-button-thread.md`: a Reddit thread captured by social listening. No ticket exists for it.

Only discuss an issue after the person has referenced its complaint. Until then, don't name that issue, its root cause, or the affected files, and don't hint at it in project overviews or summaries. Referencing one complaint doesn't unlock the other.
